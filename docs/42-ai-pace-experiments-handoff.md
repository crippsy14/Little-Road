# 42: AI pace experiments — plan and handoff

24 September 2026. Branch `ai-pace-experiments`. Started in a Cowork session; continue in a local Claude Code session.

## Why
Solo, the AI is close to human pace (Cedar club best lap 64.98 s vs Richard's 62.60 s). In the 24-car race the AI car's lap is ~80.1 s vs the human's 63.8 s in the same traffic, so ~15 s/lap is lost to traffic handling. Code review found the causes below.

## Plan, in order (one at a time, each measured)
1. **Path-aware leader limit** (`MovingLeaderControl`) — the car-ahead speed cap uses current lateral offset, so it keeps braking while the planner pulls out to pass. **Tried in 112: rejected as implemented** (see `review/unity-35/frame-cost-112-review.md`). Cedar +11.4 s worse on average; leader-capped time fell from 33–38% to 25–29% of racing but "no safe path" plans rose ~1.5% → ~2.1% and braking rose. Retry later with pass commitment/hysteresis (lift the cap only for a pass chosen consistently across several plans), on top of step 2.
2. **Gentler "no safe path" response** — `TrafficUnresolved` currently commands full brake and zero steering. Experiment `--unresolved-hold` (written in `ContextDriverPolicy.PlanCommand`, **not yet built or tested**): hold current lateral offset via a fresh `FromMotion` path, no added power; leader limit and immediate guard still brake. **Next action: build 113, run the pace study for `--unresolved-hold`, then for both flags together.** Editor check added (`UnresolvedHoldChecks.cs`, registered after `PathAwareLeaderChecks`; hold/brake responses moved to static `ContextDriverPolicy.UnresolvedHoldCommand`/`UnresolvedBrakeCommand`, behaviour unchanged). **Built and tested in 113 (543 checks): not kept** (see `review/unity-35/frame-cost-113-review.md`). Alone: Cedar −13.9 s mean, but Bayview 1709 did not finish (21/24, 10 assisted resets, stationary pile-up). With `--path-aware-leader`: Cedar −20.9 s mean, Bayview −1.8 s mean (two seeds worse, new physical recoveries). Contacts rise throughout. **Retried in 114 as `--conditional-hold` (hold only when no car in our corridor is within 3 s time-to-contact or overlapping; otherwise brake): kept together with `--path-aware-leader`** (see `review/unity-35/frame-cost-114-review.md`). 24/24 everywhere, no assisted resets, collective −18.9 s Cedar / −18.6 s Bayview mean, better on all six seeds (~0.8 s per car per lap). `--conditional-hold` alone was worse on the Bayview screen. **Promoted to default in 115**; opt out with `--no-conditional-hold` / `--no-path-aware-leader` (115 reproduces 114 exactly both ways). From 115 on, the pace-study baseline includes both.
3. **One speed decision** — merge the stacked pedal limits (follower, moving leader, unresolved brake, static scenery, immediate guard) into a single arbitration with explicit reasons; remove duplicated safety reserves after measuring which ones bind.
4. **Deliberate passing** — score candidates by progress minus personality-weighted risk; add an explicit pass set-up option instead of only lateral target offsets. **Started after measuring step 3 (see below).** 116 `--sustained-leader-ranking` (keep a followed car in the candidate ranking for the whole horizon, not 3 s): rejected at screen, Cedar +2.5 s / Bayview +6.7 s (see `review/unity-35/frame-cost-116-review.md`). Cars close faster, then get corridor-rejected and brake late.
5. **Clean-up** — group the ~96 CLI switches into named presets, move rejected/legacy policies out of the live path, keep diagnostics separate from driving logic. **Switch preset done (study 117):** `RaceAiPreset` (was `AdventureDriverPreset`, `RaceRoster.cs`) is the one AI package for adventure races and trials (`--ai-preset field`). It now includes three-group staggered planning, so **adventure races now run exactly what is tested** (before, they re-planned unstaggered). `--preset-without <switch>` drops one switch for A/B runs. `run-frame-cost.ps1` passes the preset plus race settings (78 → 46 launch arguments). Verified identical: Cedar 1709 2116.613, Bayview 1709 803.352. Inventory of the 96 switches: 31 preset, 4 opt-outs, 15 audits, ~33 trial/review/tool settings, and 10 driving experiments that are off in races (`--unresolved-hold`, `--sustained-leader-ranking`, `--third-dynamic-candidate`, `--rolling-recovery`, `--retain-pass-trajectory`, `--timed-rear-corridors`, `--path-speed-envelope`, `--integrated-passing-progress`, `--anticipate-blocking-car`, `--legacy-adventure-ai`); **Removed (study 117, 519 checks):** all ten switches are gone. `--unresolved-hold`, `--sustained-leader-ranking`, `--third-dynamic-candidate`, `--retain-pass-trajectory` (+`PassTrajectory`), `--timed-rear-corridors`, `--rolling-recovery` (+`RollingRecovery`, its probe, and the rolling parts of `PhysicalRecovery`) and `--legacy-adventure-ai` are deleted with their checks. `--path-speed-envelope`, `--integrated-passing-progress` and `--anticipate-blocking-car` no longer have switches, but their constructor options remain as comparison fixtures in editor checks. Verified identical: Cedar 1709 2116.613, Bayview 1709 803.352.

**Next: see `docs/43-ai-racing-review.md`** (a fresh-eyes review). Its top recommendation is pass commitment / intent memory: plans currently reverse sides ~24 times a minute per club car.

**Review goals (24 Sep, evening; see `docs/43-ai-racing-review.md` and reviews 118-120):** pass commitment `--pass-commitment` neutral (kept opt-in); corner-aware pass `--corner-aware-pass` rejected; gap seeking `--gap-seeking` rejected after a full study (seed 1709 was a false positive, so screen with 1709 + 1710); shoulder use `--use-shoulder` rejected badly (edge not drivable as modelled); pace margins `--pace-margins` strong on Cedar (-19.7 s) but Bayview 1710 +31.3 s (car0 off); retry with a gentler step. The passing experiments point at pack density (no free lane among slower street cars) rather than passing logic. All off by default. Paused here to work on the pre/post race screens.

**Parked — grid order.** Club cars start mostly behind the 18 street cars (car 0 from slot 23 of 24), which forces passing on the tight opening straight. Richard's idea for non-endurance races: keep the mixed grid, but put the three fastest cars in the first three slots. Needs thought on balance before changing.

**Additional focus — opening straight (Cedar sector 1).** Seen watching 115 (and before): off the grid the pack plods down the first straight at about half throttle until it spreads out at T2/T3. Watch run `review/unity-35/frame-cost-115/watch-cedar-*`, samples before gate 1 (172 m): mean pedal 0.6, full throttle only 19%, no braking. Reasons: 59% "Checked local curve and speed to shared downstream gate" (path speed cap), 40% "Moving leader stopping-distance limit". Field reaches gate 1 at 9.0 s (first) / 10.9 s (mean) / 12.7 s (last); gate 2 mean 17.9 s. `summarize-pace-study.py` now prints field-mean gate 1/gate 2 times and their paired deltas for every study. Track these for each experiment, and look at the opening directly once steps 3–4 have landed. Opening times are identical across seeds (deterministic grid), so one seed is enough to screen opening changes.
**Cause found (115 `--pedal-stage-audit`, `frame-cost-115/*-pedalaudit`):** the roster is mixed. 18/24 cars are `street` equipment (gearCeiling 25 m/s, power 187), and only 6 are `club` (ids 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20; 34 m/s, power 419). On the straight the street cars' follower target is exactly 25.0 m/s and they sit at it, so the "half throttle" is them at top speed. The club cars are boxed in behind them (moving-leader limit binds 31% before gate 1). The opening is therefore a passing problem (step 4, club cars getting past street cars), not a speed-arbitration problem.

**Step 3 measurement (115 pedal-stage audit, share of racing samples where each stage lowers the pedal, Cedar / Bayview):** moving leader 26% / 40% (mean cut 0.38 / 0.44 pedal); unresolved brake/hold 3% / 5%; immediate guard 2% / 2%; static scenery 0% / 0%. The follower is below 0.95 throttle 77% / 56% of the time, mostly holding its own target (equipment ceiling or corner envelope). So the stacked safety limits barely bind apart from the leader cap. Merging them (step 3) is mostly a clean-up with little pace in it. The pace is in the leader cap, i.e. getting past slower cars (step 4).

Also checked: using more road width for the racing line (currently about ±5 m on Cedar, ±4.3 m on Bayview, road ±8 m) gained only about 0.5 s in a rough model — not a priority.

## How to build and test
- Build: `.\tools\build-dev.ps1` (replaces `../little-road-dev-build/`; ~1 minute; expect `LITTLE_ROAD_BUILD_OK ... checks=519` after the 117 clean-up). Numbered exports are no longer made; the old ones were deleted to free space.

The script skips existing run folders, so a full run after a screen only adds the missing seeds. Use `-SkipBaseline` to add another arm to an existing study folder.
- Judging: more contacts alone is fine (racing, risk ratings); assisted resets, DNFs, pile-ups and long physical recoveries are the red flags.
- Harness changes: `run-frame-cost.ps1` gained `-Extra` (pass-through flags) and `-PlaybackRate`; `run-technical-trial.ps1` gained `-ExtraArguments`.
