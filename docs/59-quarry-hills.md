This doc is step 0 of [doc 57](57-new-area-standards.md)'s process for Quarry Hills (the brief), plus the district plan and its activities. The interactive plan is the **Quarry Hills Plan** artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/Gk3hXSFCt8PSunsZ48TiJg (private to Richard).

Its place in the world ([doc 58](58-world-and-campaign-design.md)):
- **Where:** the Spine's northern foothills, at (3.7, 16.2) km on the continent.
- **Climate:** the wet west side of the range.
- **Class:** class C, and the first gravel town.
- **Railway:** the start of the Pine & Peak railway.
- **Neighbours:** 1.7 km from Kettle Falls and 2.6 km from Tollgate.

## The vision

**"The mountain the town dug."** Quarry Hills is slate country:
- twelve giant steps cut into the hillside above a turquoise pit lake;
- Blondin cable cranes across the sky;
- a gravity slate railway down to the sea;
- grey terraced houses climbing the slope below.

The story: the quarry is winding down, and the town is betting on motorsport to keep it alive. You arrive in your first car just as it opens a rally school. By the post-game the quarry has closed, and the town is the home of rally.

**Why it isn't a second Hillcrest:**

| Hillcrest | Quarry Hills |
|---|---|
| One climb (the spiral to the castle) | Layered: down onto the quarry floor and into the lake, up the galleries to the rim and the moor |
| Still | Moving: Blondins, dumpers, slate trains, the noon blast |
| Tarmac | Gravel first, taught by a rally school |
| Sunny | Westerly drizzle, mist on the galleries, wet slate shining |
| A castle town | A railway town, with Pine & Peak's first station, engine shed and spiral |
| 2.4 × 1.6 km valley (3.9 km²) | 3.2 × 2.6 km district (8.3 km², 2.1×) |

**Arrival:** Quarry Road crests the Hillcrest ridge, and the whole stepped mountain, the pit and the town open up at once (principle 2, the triangle rule). The galleries can be seen from the west edge of Hillcrest, so they pull you here (principle 1).

**Roads:**
- Quarry Road (tarmac) from Hillcrest to the town and the quay;
- the haul road (gravel) down to the quarry floor and up the galleries to the rim;
- Old Quarry Road (gravel) south to Tollgate;
- the gravel road west to Kettle Falls;
- Pine & Peak from Slate Quay through the spiral to Kettle Falls and Fernhollow, plus a slate branch to the quarry floor.

## How we build it (doc 57 process)

1. **Landform:** author the district's ground over the generated continent, the way Hillcrest's valley is authored:
   - the galleries as real 15 m benches;
   - the pit and its lake;
   - the tips;
   - the coast and the quay.
2. **Graybox:** roads and tracks through `RoadNetwork`, then 24-car races on three seeds for every event.
3. **Gravel feel:** dirt grip is already real off the tarmac. Add:
   - loose-surface slip;
   - dust;
   - stones in the sound;
   - mud-flap and gravel-tyre parts that matter.
4. **Moving set pieces:**
   - Blondins and the zip: from the castle zipline's carrier.
   - Slate train: Beat the Train.
   - Dumpers: the valley traffic.
   - The blast: dust and rockfall, scripted, never touching the car.
5. **Town:** slate terraces from the kit; the chapel, Institute, station and engine shed, the Great Wheel, Barracks Row; the uniform Pit Stop.
6. **Discoveries last:** carvings, viewpoints, recruits, the lake secret.

**Drive tests** between towns wait until towns are populated (Richard, 3 October). The scale test stays for scale and pacing only.

## Build status (3 October, afternoon)

To get there, take Quarry Road west off the raceway road, over the river, through the gap in the west hills and past the source tarn. At the crest the town and quarry open up below you.

### Next for Quarry Hills

- **Ride and races:** make the Blondin a ride, reusing the ZipLine carrier (ZipLine is still a single instance). Add the Glas zip. Grid the Quarry Sprint for 24 cars on three seeds.
- **Rally school and rally parts:** build the six lessons, with gold unlocking the rally parts. Then the Heavy Haul dumper job and the Blast Run.
- **Keepsakes:** the 12 slate carvings on the gallery faces (the keepsake count is still 0).
- **Roads and railway:** the haul road's switchbacks up the galleries to the rim and moor (Gallery Climb), and the Pine & Peak line from the quay.
- **Town:** the Great Wheel, and more lamps and furniture to reach Hillcrest's density.
- **Edge audit:** the last lip and two banks, at the Terrace Row and Chapel Street junction corners.
