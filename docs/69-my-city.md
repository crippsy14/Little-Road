# 69 · My City: your own city (plan, to return to)

Richard, 7 October: "I think that when you find it, it should be run down and empty, but you are gifted it as your city space. You can then purchase to flatten certain areas and then find people through the world that want to join your city, like the original."

He continued: "The difference being that when you find someone that wants to be in your city, you have to pay for their land (one time purchase, can be moved and removed then readded for free) in a city planner view, where you can add your own roads, traffic lights, decorations etc etc." And: "My City has huge potential."

"The original" is Road Trip Adventure (Choro Q HG 4). There, your home town grows as people you meet on the road move in.

## Where it sits

In [doc 58](58-world-and-campaign-design.md), My City is:
- the hub on the dam at Glasswater's northern end, at (11.0, 12.6) km;
- class A's home;
- the start of Grand Tour A;
- the way down to Lowtown.

It's 8.8 km from Bayview.

## The idea

1. **Found run down.**
   - You arrive to empty streets, boarded shops, cracked roads and weeds.
   - The dam and its ramp to Lowtown are still there.
   - You're gifted the town as **your city space**.
2. **Clear the ground.** Buy the right to flatten areas of the old town, plot by plot, to make room.
3. **Find people.**
   - Residents across the world want to move to your city: recruits, shopkeepers, characters from the towns and villages.
   - Each one is found through the world: a conversation, a challenge, a favour.
4. **Pay for their land.**
   - A one-time purchase per resident.
   - After that, their building can be moved, removed and re-added for free.
5. **The city planner view.** A top-down planner where you place:
   - residents' buildings;
   - your own roads;
   - traffic lights;
   - decorations.

   The city then lives: traffic on your roads, residents at their doors.
