## What to record when reviewing footage

1. **Driving:** steering at different speeds, braking, off-road grip, jumps, recovery and camera movement.
2. **Navigation:** signs, landmarks, optional paths, town-to-road transitions and how new routes become apparent.
3. **Progression:** the exact action that grants a part, unlocks a race or recruits a resident.
4. **Equipment:** the visible and behavioural difference before and after a swap.
5. **Presentation:** car-to-building scale, silhouettes, texture density, readable dialogue and sound cues.
6. **Pacing:** how long ordinary travel lasts before a discovery, interaction or meaningful choice.

For each observation, record the video URL, timestamp, what can actually be seen and any interpretation separately.

## Our original concept references

The project contains two exploratory AI-generated concept sheets made with the built-in image-generation tool. These express possible art directions; they are not screenshots from Road Trip Adventure, model blueprints, implemented features or promises of final rendering quality.

- [Town and road concept](../references/concepts/coastal-world.png): a coastal village, scenic route, garage interaction and small race.
- [Vehicle and equipment concept](../references/concepts/vehicle-equipment.png): a consistent compact car with road, trail and speculative water-travel configurations.

The water equipment is an original proposal. Its illustrated flotation pods should not be mistaken for a claim about the original game's propeller behaviour. Generated vehicle views may need corrections for consistent modelling dimensions and topology.

Both images have been generated, visually inspected and saved under `references/concepts/`, together with their [exact prompts and review notes](../references/concepts/generation-prompts.md). The world sheet is more detailed than the first prototype should be; its composition and mood are the useful references.
