# Racing-line pilots and recorded human laps

21 September 2026. Phase B is in progress; the broad goal remains active.

The offline baker creates centre, bounded minimum-curvature, half-offset, and early/late shifted candidates with a source-track hash. Runtime profiles use actual candidate path curvature, backward braking and forward acceleration feasibility. The solo reference driver sends ordinary pedal/steering commands through the shared car physics. Candidate overlays distinguish the path, braking/acceleration, ±1 m corridor and road centre. These remain engineering fixtures, not campaign AI or an accepted ideal line.

Build 01 passed 217 editor checks. The two club-car, two-lap pilot runs in `unity-29/pilot-centre-club` and `pilot-mincurve-club` produced flying laps of 68.750 s and 66.334 s respectively, a 2.416 s improvement for the candidate under the same controller. Both had zero contacts, recoveries, missed gates and sampled full-body departures. Evidence is sampled at 10 Hz, not continuous proof of collision clearance. Candidate time-weighted ±1 m adherence was 93.94%; distance-weighted was 95.35%. Repeated runs, stock equipment, held-out starts and the remaining candidate comparisons are still required before selecting a reference.

`Record Your Cedar Laps.cmd` opens a separate four-lap club-car session. Telemetry labels human/AI input ownership; the human recording is comparison evidence, never automatic training. The first lap can be excluded as familiarisation. Reports save on completion, Escape or normal application close; forced termination is not guaranteed to save. These runs do not alter adventure saves.

The new display option supports borderless fullscreen at the current display resolution, F11 switching, an adventure pause-menu choice and a clickable trial button. User choice is remembered; headless tests do not change it. The recorded-lap launcher starts fullscreen. Trial HUD/map dimensions scale from a 1600×900 reference so larger displays retain readable text. The desktop adventure remains checkpoint 20 until a separately verified promotion; use the recorded-lap launcher for these changes.
