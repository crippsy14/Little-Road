## Overall impression
Quay road is the strongest part of the town. The façades are varied and well coloured, with flower boxes, shutters, arcades and awnings. The bunting across the road, the café, the post office with its pillar box, and the Pit Stop and zipline plaza give it real life. It mostly looks good. The recurring problems are placement hygiene (cars parked against doors and overlapping each other, shop names hidden behind railings), and the river edge, which the street is named after, is under-dressed. Station road and the town edges feel empty: wide lawns with nothing on them.
