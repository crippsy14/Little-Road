# Bramblewick Lanes, launch review round 4

## LR3 items
- LR3-01 (cropped corner and warning boards): FIXED. "RIM CORNER" (toplane) and "HUMPBACK BRIDGE" (bridge) now fit inside their boards. The boards stand above the hedge and sit just off the shoulder.
- LR3-02 (FORD boards too small): not clearly visible in the ford or ford-close frames. I could not confirm it. Unverified.
- LR3-03 (gantry text and bunting): FIXED. "BRAMBLEWICK LANES" is readable and the bunting sits well clear of the gantry (start frame).
- LR3-04 (sausage-like hedge-bank end stubs): PARTLY FIXED. The rounded hedge ends are still visible in rookery, hedgegap and gallows. They read as clipped topiary and are acceptable, but still slightly sausage-like.
- LR3-05 (beacon flame): UNVERIFIABLE. In the beacon frame it is a tiny orange tip on a grey tower at long range. It no longer reads as a ball or silo, but I can't judge the three tongues at this distance.

## New findings
- LR4-01 (polish), toplane and hedgegap: the sign text is mid-green on pale grey, so contrast is low and it is hard to read at speed. Fix: use near-black or very dark green text, or a white board.
- LR4-02 (polish), toplane: the left-hand drystone wall has a row of thin vertical spikes along its top edge. It looks like a texture or mesh seam. Fix: cap the wall with a coping strip or fix the top-row UVs.
- LR4-03 (polish), start: the left-hand flag pole is a bare grey spike. Its flag is cropped or off-frame, and it is taller than the other poles. Fix: add a flag or shorten it.
- LR4-04 (polish), race-03 to race-07: the hay-bale rows at the track edge are lined up neatly and read as fixed props. This is fine, but some bales sit very close to the kerb at the hairpin. Not a defect, only a note.
- LR4-05 (polish), esses, dairyT, ford: the verges are bare, flat grass with no props. The track reads as clean but a little empty. Fix: add a few kilometre posts, a gate or a milestone.

## Checked and clean
- Ford: the water strip has no gaps.
- Kerbs: the red and white kerbs have no seams.
- Chase-cam frames: no clipping and no z-fighting. The 24-car field is readable.
- Bridge, millbend, cider, bank-close: no obstacles in the run-off and no drops beside the road.

The track is fun-looking, readable and finished enough. The only remaining items are polish.
