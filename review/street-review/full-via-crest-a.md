# Via Crest (Hillcrest) street review, part A (stops 005 to 012)

Photos reviewed: 23 of 23 (005-left, 005-right, and 006 to 012 along/left/right). None unreadable.

Folder: review/quarry-hills/streets-1/streets/hillcrest/via-crest/

Checked and not reported (known false alarms): lamps standing road-side of parked cars (010-right, 008-right, 005-right), chimney smoke puffs, and buildings at different depths that look merged.

## Defects

**VCA-1** (005-right, 006-along, 006-right, 007-right, 011-right, 012-right)
Where a house or block backs onto a slope, the grass rises across the side wall at a steep diagonal. The wall base runs up at an angle, so the far half of the building looks buried or leaning. Clearest in 007-right (yellow block, centre-left), 011-right (yellow house, centre) and 012-right (yellow house, right, with a diagonal base line). Also visible in 006-along (red building, right) and 006-right (red building, far left).
Severity: medium. Confidence: medium.

**VCA-2** (006-right, 007-left, 008-left, 008-right, 009-left, 010-right, 011-right, 012-right)
The top-floor loggias are solid black rectangles behind two stone arches, with coloured polyhedra sitting along the sill. They read as holes in the wall, or as gems floating in a void, rather than as an open room or terrace. Seen at upper-centre or upper-right on many buildings, repeated every few houses.
Severity: medium. Confidence: high for the black fill, medium for "unintended".

**VCA-3** (005-right, 009-right, 010-right, 011-right, 012-right)
Parked cars sit across the pavement edge and the grass strip, and several stop right against a building front or across a doorway.
- 005-right: the blue car is on the pavement in front of a shop door, nose to the wall, with the awning above it (centre-left).
- 011-right: the teal van is in front of a door, wheels on the pavement, and its rear corner looks to be inside the window flower box on the wall (centre).
- 009-right: the cream SUV is pressed against the stone planter and the window box beside it (right).
- 012-right: the white SUV is fully on the pavement paving and its nose runs into the base of the building (centre-right).
Severity: medium. Confidence: medium for the pavement and doorway placement; low to medium for the actual clipping at 011-right, 009-right and 012-right (possibly camera angle).

**VCA-4** (005-left, 006-left, 008-left, 009-left, 010-left)
On sloping plots a grey stone plinth wedge hangs under the building where the lawn drops away, so the house looks propped up over a gap. In 005-left (centre) the yellow shop sits on a bare grey wedge above the grass. In 010-left (centre) the orange block's plinth stands over an open drop to the vineyard terraces. The knee-high pavement wall beside it is short and stepped. In 008-left the wall has a gap by the lamp, and in 005-left the wall segments are misaligned, with the left end floating. The pavement stays between the drop and the road, so this is a low safety concern but looks unfinished.
Severity: low to medium. Confidence: medium.

**VCA-5** (012-left)
The gabled terracotta roof (upper centre-right) shows wavy contour or moire swirls across the tiles, as if the texture is aliasing or two surfaces are overlapping. The neighbouring roofs are clean.
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

**VCA-6** (005-right, 006-right, 007-right, 009-left, 011-left)
Shop name signs on the first-floor balconies are partly hidden behind the railing and flower boxes, so the text is cut off or unreadable. In 006-right "PIZZA" has its upper edge clipped by the railing. In 009-left "WINE" is cut and half off the frame edge. In 011-left "OLIVES" is hidden behind the rail. In 005-left "HATS" is mostly covered by the railing and flowers. In 005-right "KITES" and a second sign are blurry behind the rail.
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

**VCA-7** (006-right, 007-right)
Door steps and loose stone slabs lie tilted on the grass, away from the door. In 006-right (lower centre-right) a flat slab sits on the lawn at an angle. In 007-right (centre, below the blue door) a wedge-shaped step sticks up from the grass.
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

**VCA-8** (011-left, 012-left)
There are narrow gaps between neighbouring houses where the end walls do not meet. A sliver of terrain, shrubs and vineyard shows through (011-left, centre-left; 012-left, between the green block and the peach house).
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

**VCA-9** (007-right, 009-right, 011-left, 012-left)
Stone planters stand directly against window flower boxes and merge with them (007-right, lower right of the red house; 011-left, left; 012-left, centre). A bench in 011-left sits right under a window box. The planter, hedge and flower box overlap and form one clump.
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

## Improvements (brief)

- Give the loggias a lit interior: a floor, a back wall and furniture or plants, or fill them in.
- Add a proper terrace or garden wall so that the plinth wedges and the drops beside them are hidden.
- Tone down the repeated building types. The same black-loggia house, the same cream awning shop and the same terracotta house with an identical door recur along the street.
- Shutters on adjacent windows touch or overlap on the long fronts (005-right orange house; 010-left and 010-right).
- Move some parked cars off the strip in front of doors so each house has a clear path to the pavement.
- The stair-stepped shadow on the road (011-along, 012-along) is blocky. A softer cascade would read better.
