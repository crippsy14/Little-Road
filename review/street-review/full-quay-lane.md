## Defects

**QL2-1** (005-right, 006-left, 006-right, 009-left, 010-left)
Upper-floor arched loggia on the taller grey/beige houses is solid black inside the two arches. There is no back wall or interior, so it reads as a hole in the facade with five coloured gems floating in front. Seen in the upper-centre or upper-right of the frame in each photo.
Severity: medium. Confidence: high.

**QL2-2** (005-left, 005-right, 006-left, 007-left, 008-left, 008-right, 009-right)
Shop name signs ("CHIPS", "HARDWARE", "SLATE CRAFTS", "CHEMIST", "BAKERY", "WOOL") sit directly behind the first-floor balcony railing and flower boxes. The railing bars and flowers cut through the letters and the end letters are cropped (e.g. "RDWARE", "RAKERY", "WOOL" with the first letter lost), so most signs are hard to read. In 005-right the sign on the nearest shop may read "LIVERAIL", which I cannot make out (low confidence).
Severity: medium. Confidence: high.

**QL2-3** (009-left far left, 010-right centre)
Large arched shopfront opening has a dark void with a flat blue panel that does not fill the arch, and a separate door beside it. The arch stones on the right side are jagged and misaligned (a loose block sticks out at the arch edge). In 010-right it is clearly visible at the centre; in 009-left it is at the far left edge.
Severity: medium. Confidence: medium-high.

**QL2-4** (006-right, 007-left, 009-left, 010-right)
Small benches are pushed up under ground-floor windows. The flower box above overlaps the bench backrest, and the bench sits on the narrow strip against the plinth. They read as squeezed in or partly inside the wall. Seen at lower centre of the building row. Bench in 006-right is at the middle-lower centre, 007-left at lower centre, 009-left twice (centre and centre-right), 010-right at lower left.
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

**QL2-5** (011-left, 011-right, 012-along, 010-along)
Low roadside stone walls on the open verges are broken up. Segments are tilted at different angles, have visible gaps, and in 011-left (right side) one piece looks to be sinking into the ground. In 012-along there are a few dark box-shaped blocks beside the road in the far right that look like stray wall pieces. No steep drop is involved; the wall only runs beside flat fields.
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

**QL2-6** (011-left, 011-right, 012-left)
Pale teal-blue patches and streaks lie on the grass of the fields (centre and left middle ground). They look like stray terrain paint or decals that do not match the grass.
Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

**QL2-7** (011-left, 012-left)
Trees on the far hillside show dark flat discs above or beside the canopies, so they look like they have floating black caps. Far background, upper-centre.
Severity: low. Confidence: low (distance makes it hard to tell if it is the tree model or its shadow).

**QL2-8** (011-along, 012-along)
A faint tone change across the road surface in the far distance (around the middle of the road in 011-along) suggests a seam where road sections meet.
Severity: low. Confidence: low.

## Improvements (brief)

- 000-along: the plaza paving is a large bare area. A few more props (planters, a fountain or stalls) would break it up.
- 010-along and 011-along: the town ends abruptly at a bare end wall and then open fields. A gateway, hedge or planting would make the edge feel intentional.
- The open fields in 011 and 012 are very flat and bare apart from sparse grass tufts. A few trees, hedges or sheep near the road would help.
- Facades repeat the same few templates (shutter colours, loggia, awning) along the whole lane. More variation in roof shapes and heights would help.
- No cars or people appear along the lane, so the street feels empty.
