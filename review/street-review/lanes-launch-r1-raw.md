# Bramblewick Lanes: launch review r1 (sheet + start, ford, hedgegap, hairpin, bridge at full size)

Verdict: NOT YET. The road, hedge banks, kerbs and village start read well, but the ford looks broken, the lap is visually repetitive, and several features the brief promises (brook, humpback bridge, hay-bale walls, spectators) are not visible in the views.

## Defects
- LR1-01 | ford | HIGH (conf. high) | The "ford" is a set of flat, pale-blue, overlapping rectangles laid on the road. Edges are ragged and stepped, and white edge strips poke out. It sits above the tarmac and spills onto the grass both sides. There is no brook, banks or reeds, and it is not stone-edged. It looks like a placeholder, and the stepped sheets could read as a bump or a hole. A depth gauge post stands at right.
- LR1-02 | bridge | HIGH (conf. medium) | No humpback bridge or crest is visible. The road is flat, with ordinary wall-and-hedge corridors either side. The brief's "humpback bridge" is missing or invisible from the line.
- LR1-03 | bridge | MEDIUM (conf. high) | A "MILL BEND" board in the far distance (right) is mirrored, seen from behind, with speckled particle dots around it. It is unreadable from the approach.
- LR1-04 | bridge, hairpin, hedgegap | MEDIUM (conf. medium) | The dry-stone wall texture is blocky. The top course looks like a row of vertical planks (picket-fence). Wall ends are abrupt, with doubled wall stubs and open gaps (bridge, right at the gate; hairpin, left). Hedge tubes end in curled "sausage" stubs. This is a toy look but reads as unfinished.
- LR1-05 | hedgegap | MEDIUM (conf. medium) | The inside-left stone block with a hedge "sausage" and a tree behind sits right at the kerb. It is a chunky monolith that looks pasted on, and it hides the corner exit. The kerb start is also a plain slab (white block, no taper).
- LR1-06 | start | MEDIUM (conf. high) | The gantry text "BRAMBLEWICK LANES" is blue-grey on mustard, with low contrast and poor legibility at speed. The grid slot marks are thin crosses and lines that look like debug marks. The start line is a faint dashed strip under the gantry. There is a centre line on the village street, which is fine.
- LR1-07 | hairpin | MEDIUM (conf. medium) | The "hairpin" view looks like a long straight toward the grandstand. The hairpin itself is not readable. There is no braking board, bale wall or run-off in view. The grandstand sits dead ahead, so it can look like the road continues. This is a possible unfair surprise (the bale walls from the brief are not visible).
- LR1-08 | toplane, beacon, gallows | LOW (conf. low) | Possible shadow band or seam on the road near the left lane in toplane (a thin dark diagonal). Not confirmed.

## Readability
- Kerbs (hedgegap, cider, churchhill, orchard) are the best readability tool, and the red/white is clear. Tighter corners without kerbs (esses, barn, rookery, toplane) hide their exits behind hedges, so corner tightness is hard to judge.
- Corner boards and braking markers are absent everywhere I can see.

## Atmosphere and variety
- Eleven of the twenty views (esses to millbend) show the same corridor: stone bank, hedge, grey road, beige verge. They are only distinguishable by a lone tree. This is the main monotony risk.
- Positive: the village start (thatch, flags, lamps, hedge clumps), the orchard grandstand and hay bales, the sky and the rolling hills. The aerials show a good field patchwork.
- Race-day dressing is thin: no spectators, no flags or banners along the lane, and few signs. One grandstand and a few bale strings is not enough for "race day".

## Top improvements (priority order)
1. Rebuild the ford as a real brook: a recessed water plane with stone banks and wing walls, a shallow road dip, foam or spray, hedge breaks, and a "FORD" warning sign. (ford)
2. Make the humpback bridge an actual crest of about 1.5 to 2 m with stone parapets, so it is a visible landmark and a mistake generator. (bridge)
3. Break up the hedge corridor with field gates, gaps looking onto fields, a farm barn or tractor, a pub, a phone box, telegraph poles, and varied hedge heights. Add a one-off landmark per section: a church tower, mill or oast. (esses to millbend)
4. Add corner boards (100/50 m) and bale walls at the hairpin, the Dairy T and the cider corner. Add spectator groups and bunting at the hairpin, the ford and the start. (hairpin, dairyT)
5. Fix the wall texture caps and ends: use a proper coping course, with tapered end piers and no open stubs. Fix or mirror the Mill Bend board. (hedgegap, bridge)
6. Improve the gantry text contrast (dark green on cream), and use proper grid boxes. (start)
