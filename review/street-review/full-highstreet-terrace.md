# Quarry Hills street review: High Street and Terrace Row (streets-1)

Photos reviewed: 24 of 24 (high-street 007 along/left/right; terrace-row 000 to 006, each along/left/right). Unreadable: none.

Checked against the known false alarms (lamps beside cars, chimney smoke puffs, buildings at different depths). None of those are reported below.

## High Street (HS-)

HS-1. high-street 007-right (also visible in the distance, 007-along). In the upper right, the top-floor loggia of the pink/brown building has two arches that open onto a flat black void, so it looks like a missing wall or hole in the facade. The flower balls float in front of the black. Severity: medium. Confidence: high.

HS-2. high-street 007-left. At the far left, the ground-floor window flower box sits directly on top of a bench pushed against the wall under the window, so the box and bench overlap. Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

HS-3. high-street 007-left, 007-along. Possible only. The green pick-up's tail and bed sit hard against the curved garden wall at the left, and the wall may be clipping the bodywork. Severity: low. Confidence: low.

HS-4. high-street 007-right, 007-along. Possible only. The red hatchback (007-right, centre) and the red car at the far right edge of 007-along are parked half on the pavement with their wheels on the verge, and the front of the red hatchback runs into the end of the low wall. This may be intended as kerb parking, but it reads as a car on the pavement. Severity: low. Confidence: low.

## Terrace Row (TR-)

TR-1. terrace-row 001-right, 002-along, 002-left, 003-right, 004-right, 005-right, 005-along. The top-floor loggia on the pink/grey terrace houses is a black void behind two stone arches. It is repeated on many houses and is very obvious in the close-ups (004-right centre-right, 005-right right, 001-right centre). It looks like a hole through the building. Severity: medium. Confidence: high.

TR-2. terrace-row 001-left, 003-right, 004-left, 005-left, 000-along. The shop-name signs (SWEETS, LLYFRAU, STOP, BOSS, CHIPS, CHEMIST, HARDWARE) sit on the balcony fascia and are partly hidden behind the balcony rail, flower balls and shutters. The text is cut off, for example "SWEETS" (001-left, centre), "LLYFRAU" (003-right, right; 005-left, left), "STOP" (004-left, left) and "CHIPS" (005-left, centre). In 000-along the CHEMIST sign is almost unreadable from the road. Severity: medium. Confidence: high.

TR-3. terrace-row 003-right, 006-right, 003-left, 001-left. The stone arches around the ground-floor shop and house entrances have loose, displaced blocks at the springing points. Chunks stick out of the arch at the sides (003-right, at both arches, centre; 006-right, left and centre) and the arch edge looks jagged. Inside the arch is a flat blue panel against black. Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

TR-4. terrace-row 002-left, 002-along, 000-left. The stepped, zig-zag low stone walls on the front lawns are odd. In 002-left (centre) a jagged free-standing wall wanders across the lawn and does not enclose or retain anything. In 002-along (left) the wall is stepped and floats on the slope. In 000-left (centre-left) a curved wall is stacked as separate offset blocks on the corner lawn. Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

TR-5. terrace-row 000-right, 006-left, 005-right, 003-left. Benches are placed tight against house fronts. In 000-right (centre-right) a window flower box rests on a bench against the wall, in 006-left (bottom left) the bench backs onto a window box and sits in front of a door, and in 005-right the bench is jammed up against a door step. They read as clipping with the facade and as blocking the doorway. Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

TR-6. terrace-row 002-right. Possible only. The detached house in the centre sits on a plinth that sinks into the slope on the left but stands proud at the right, where the potted bush on the lower right corner seems to hover at the plinth edge. The ground-floor arches also show dark voids behind the windows. Severity: low. Confidence: low.

TR-7. terrace-row 000-left. The give-way sign on the lawn is seen from behind, so it shows a blank grey triangle and no symbol. In 006-along it is the right way round. Severity: low. Confidence: medium.

TR-8. terrace-row 006-left, 001-right, 004-right. Possible only. Some cream door-step slabs overhang the sloping grass and look slightly detached from the plinth (006-left, centre, below the purple door). Severity: low. Confidence: low.

## Improvements (brief)

- Shop windows are plain blank cream panels with no goods or lettering; they read as blank boards at close range.
- Awning scallop discs are very large and chunky, especially at 000 and 005-left, and cover part of the shop door.
- Terrace Row has no parked cars or people along its length, so it feels empty next to High Street. A few parked cars would help.
- Big blank gable and side walls (000-left, 000-right, 006-along) are flat and plain. Add a window, a drainpipe or a mural.
- The road shadows from the buildings have a stair-stepped edge (001-along, 003-along). A softer shadow setting would fix it.
- Front lawns are bare grass strips; a few hedges or fences would break them up.
