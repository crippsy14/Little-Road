# Vinelands: design concepts (21 Oct 2026)

Binding from doc 68: Douro/Portuguese flavour; a real staggered-solo hill climb; the old roadster reward at the far-bank quinta. No activities proposed here. Doc 68's human touches (pickers, treaders, a parade) must become cars (doc "world of cars").

## 1. Identity
Three pitches:
- **A. "The hill that holds the sun"** (doc 68): a whole hillside of terraces above a river bend.
- **B. "The town you read from across the water"**: the place is a slope to look at and then climb, a stage with the river as the stalls.
- **C. "A hundred years of one road"**: a harvest town that grew round its hill climb. Kilometre stones, record plaques and painted lines are the civic furniture.

**Pick: B, with C as its story.** The amphitheatre is seen from the far bank and the river road first, and then you drive up into it.
- **Bayview:** a harbour island, flat to the sea, viewed from the quay.
- **Hillcrest:** a vertical spire town, climbed inwards to a castle.
- **Quarry Hills:** a pit seen from above, grey and wet.
- **Bramblewick:** enclosed, green and low, with hedges to the eyebrows.
- **Vinelands:** open, lit and horizontal. Contour-following stripes, a wide river and long sight lines.
- **Driving feel:** you drive along the contours (sweepers) and across them (hairpins). The others are corridors or spirals.

## 2. Landmarks and set pieces
- **The Amphitheatre:** about 50 terrace benches in curved schist-walled stripes, with three quinta names in giant white letters. Reads from Hillcrest's hills and is the arrival shot. It is the contour map made visible.
- **The Switchback Stack:** the six hairpins stacked on one face of the amphitheatre. From the Cais you can see the road you will climb, and on the climb you see the cars above and below. This is the climb's identity.
- **The Iron Bridge with a train:** a lattice span at the river bend, timed so a train crosses on arrival. It frames the view and gives the lower road a crossing to pass under and look at.
- **The Cais:** a stone quay with moored rabelos, painted wine lodges and the tiled station. It is the climb's start paddock and the lowest point.
- **The Belvedere tower:** a white bell tower on a plane-tree balcony. It is the finish line, the skyline marker and the "you can see it from everywhere" pull.
- **The Adega:** a co-op yard with a barrel-vault arch and cask-lined walls. It is the Loop's start/finish and paddock, and the cobbled yard is a natural chicane-free straight.
- **The Cork Grove:** a shaded grove of stripped, ochre-trunked oaks on the back slope. It is dappled light and the gravel shortcut to Orchard End, and the contrast with the open terraces.
- **The far-bank mortórios:** an abandoned estate with collapsed terraces, seen from the Belvedere and reached over the bridge. It gives the place a quiet, wild edge and holds the roadster cellar.
- **The Calçada waves:** black-and-white cobble mosaics in wave patterns across the Largo and the climb's lower streets. They are an original graphic motif and readable from above.

## 3. Town layout
- **Three tiers, one staircase.**
  - The Cais at the river, 0 m.
  - The vila on the ridge shoulder, about 100 m.
  - The Belvedere on top, 170 m or more.
  - One road threads all three. There are no parallel alternatives that kill the climb.
- **Street pattern:**
  - A spine street (Rua Direita) runs along the ridge crest.
  - Steep stepped lanes drop off it, perpendicular to the contours like a ladder.
  - Terraces below, a stone-walled cutting at the town edge.
- **Arrival:** Valley Road South comes through a walled cutting from Bramblewick and then bursts into the whole amphitheatre. A cutting then an open reveal is the one deliberate "gasp" moment.
- **Pit Stop:** at the town gate on Valley Road, with the view in front of it. It is the first thing the player stops at and sees the valley behind.
- **Race hubs:**
  - The Adega yard (Loop paddock) sits on the Vale side, behind the Largo.
  - The Cais paddock (hill climb) sits at the river, with the timing arch across the quay.
- **Free-roam road character:**
  - *River Road:* flow, long sweepers, wall on one side and water on the other.
  - *Terrace road:* switchbacks, each hairpin a new reveal, miradouro pull-outs on the outer apexes.
  - *Vale lanes:* narrow and shaded, gravel at the edges, quick and rolling.
  - *Station arch:* a short railway underpass for a light-to-dark-to-light beat.

## 4. The two races

### Vineyard Loop (club, ~1.6 km, class C, 3 laps, ~9 m wide)
Use the tight/country band: about 5 corners per km (8 corners), 400 to 450 degrees per km, one hairpin, longest straight 0.12 to 0.18 of the lap, 4 to 5 braking zones.
- **Character:** a compact back-slope circuit with stone walls, a cork grove and a cobbled yard. It is quick, rhythmic and a little mean at the walls.
- **Elevation:** about 14 m of fall from the Adega to the dip, then a climb up the bench. The lap uses the land and never goes flat.
- **Sections:**
  - *Adega Straight:* about 220 m through the cobbled yard under the arch. It is the longest straight and the drafting zone, falling away at the end.
  - *Cooperage Hairpin:* a tight right after the downhill brake into the cellar yard. **The main passing place.**
  - *Cork Run:* three medium bends alternating in hand through the oaks. Dappled light, flowing, no overtakes needed.
  - *Press Dip:* a compression over a levada (irrigation channel) culvert, then a light crest. The car goes light and it sets up the next corner.
  - *Wall Esses:* a tight left-right-left between dry-stone walls. One line. It is the mistake generator.
  - *Long Bench:* about 250 m along a terrace bench, gently curving, wall on one side, drop on the other. It is the second overtake zone with views out.
  - *Lagar Bend:* a tightening right after a fast kink past the quinta gateposts. It is the late-lap secondary pass before the line.
- **Overtaking:** Cooperage Hairpin (main), Lagar Bend (second), a draft on Long Bench. Keep to the 9 m lane that Bramblewick proved.

### Vinelands Hill Climb (pro, ~3.8 km, point to point, solo, 10 s stagger, A/S/E)
Use the hill-climb band: 5 to 10 corners per km, 450 to 900 degrees per km, 15 to 60 m radii dominate, 6 to 8 m wide.
- **Gradient flag:** 170 m over 3.8 km averages 4.5%, below the 5 to 10% band. Aim for at least 230 m (about 6%), with the lower half near 2% and the terraces near 8 to 9%. The ridge may need to be taller.
- **Character:** speed at the bottom, precision in the middle, nerve at the top, and a payoff view at the finish.
- **Sections:**
  - *Cais Start (0 to 0.2 km):* the timing arch on the quay, setts, rabelos alongside, rolling start.
  - *Rabelo Reach (0.2 to 1.3 km):* the River Road, long linked sweepers (R 120 to 300 for class A), flat-out with the wall on the right. Time is made here.
  - *Station Arch (1.3 km):* a short railway underpass, dark then light, kink at the exit. It is the one blind moment on the flat.
  - *Lodge Corner (1.4 km):* a 90 degree right between the wine lodges that turns the road onto the terraces.
  - *The Terrace Stack (1.5 to 2.9 km):* six hairpins, each different, 80 to 200 m between them.
    - *Letter Hairpin:* wide and open under the giant painted quinta name.
    - *Cellar Cut:* blind inside a wall cutting, the tightest.
    - *Press Hairpin:* a double apex.
    - *Monorail Hairpin:* crossed by the vineyard rack railway.
    - *Outer Hairpin:* off-camber with the miradouro pull-out on the outside.
    - *Last Hairpin:* a long, tightening left onto the bench.
  - *Long Bench (2.9 to 3.3 km):* about 400 m, the only breather, at 8%. The view opens left.
  - *The Lip (3.3 km):* a blind brow where the road lifts over the terrace edge. A modest hop for fast cars (light and unsettled, not a spectacle), then a left sweeper.
  - *Belvedere Avenue (3.5 to 3.8 km):* flat-out between plane trees up to the bell tower. The finish is at the balcony.
- **Finish view:** as you cross the line the whole Switchback Stack and the river are behind and below you. You see the road you just climbed. At dusk (S) lanterns light the walls.
- **S/E variants:** more 150 to 300 m corners on the River Reach, keep hairpins at R 15 to 30.

## 5. Ambient life (cars only)
- **Wine tankers** and **barrel trucks** with painted barrel sides on River Road.
- **Tractors** with grape trailers on the terrace lanes, dust plumes behind them and slow traffic on the Vale lanes.
- **Monorail carts** (toy rack-railway cars) on the steep walls, running up and down.
- **A yellow tram** on a short quay line between the station and the Cais.
- **Three-wheeled farm vans** (Ape-style) and small blue delivery vans in the town.
- **Resident cars parked** in the Largo and at the cafe, honking at the hour.
- **The harvest train** on the Main Line, timed for the bridge view.
- **Rabelo boats** on the river, with storks on the bell tower.

## 6. Colour and material palette
- **Walls:** schist grey-brown dry stone for terraces, whitewash that grades to cream, ochre and ruby-port edging.
- **Tiles:** blue-and-white azulejo panels on the station and the church front, with some yellow accents. Original patterns, not copies of Pinhao's.
- **Roofs:** terracotta Roman tile, slightly sun-bleached.
- **Doors and trim:** green and ruby, azulejo blue. Strong rather than pastel.
- **Vines:** lime at bud-break, deep green in summer, then harvest gold, amber and rust-red. The C era sits at harvest, with S at dusk.
- **Ground:** dust ochre, cork trunks red-brown, olives silver-green, river olive-teal.
- **All colours** go through `TrialWorld.Material` and `WorldLook`, so whitewash will land as warm cream.

## 7. Risks
- **Reads as Hillcrest 2 (Italian hill town).** Avoid cypress-and-terracotta as the lead. Lead with schist stripes, blue tile and the river.
- **Terrace monotony.** Fifty identical stripes become wallpaper. Vary wall height, bench width, steps, tiny chapels, trees and a few abandoned benches.
- **Six identical hairpins.** Give each a different shape, view and camber, as above.
- **A Pinhao copy.** Invent the town, quintas, tile patterns and stations. Use the principles (stacked terraces, quay, tiled station), not the layout.
- **The lower climb is just River Road.** If the River Road Sprint (doc 68) also exists, make the sprint a visibly different event (reverse direction, or night) so the climb isn't a rerun.
- **Second C race undefined.** Doc 81 gives Vinelands 2 C races but only two tracks. Decide the second (the Loop in reverse, or a daytime variant).
- **Humans in doc 68.** Pickers, treaders and the parade are people. Make them cars.
- **Frame cost.** About 50 benches in view needs its own budget study (doc 68 step 3).
