## Haul Road (HR2-)

- Photos: haul-road 002-right, 003-left, 003-right, 004-left, 004-right, 005-left, 005-right

It reads "TOOLS" (002-right), "CAFET" and "BARA" (003-left, 004-right), "PTFS" (003-right), "STOP" (004-right), and "HARDWARE" and "SLATE CRAFTS" with the bottoms cropped (005-left). Centre of frame, near to mid distance, on every shopfront.
- Severity: medium. Confidence: high (that the text is occluded or cropped). Low for whether the words are meant to be different.

### HR2-2  Hard straight seam across the road at the first junction
- Photos: haul-road 003-along (and 003-left, right side)

It looks like two road materials butted together rather than a deliberate change.

## Chapel Street (CS2-)

### CS2-1  Sandy path laid flat on the embankment, with hard rectangular edges and a break
- Photos: chapel-street 006-left (right of centre)
- A flat beige strip with straight, angular edges runs up the grass bank from the pavement. It ends abruptly part way up (about 400 px down) and a second patch appears higher up (310-350 px down). It looks like a sheet laid on the hill, not a path that follows the terrain.
- Severity: medium. Confidence: high.

### CS2-2  Stone slabs jutting out of the retaining walls and floating over the slope
- Photos: chapel-street 009-left (upper left and mid right)
- A thin flat grey slab sticks out horizontally from the left retaining wall (110-475 px across, about 350 px down). Another sticks out beside the right-hand building's wall (1220-1340 px across, about 415 px down). Each casts a hard shadow on the slope below, so they read as floating ledges. At the top left, a bench-like slat box hangs below the pink house's window box over the wall edge.
- Severity: medium. Confidence: medium-high.

## Improvements (brief)

- Shopfronts open straight onto grass lawns with no paved forecourt, and the pale-cream shop windows are blank flat panels, so the shop rows look unfinished (Haul Road 002-005).
- The Haul Road surface (pale sandy, no markings) looks nothing like Chapel Street's marked asphalt. A short transition or centre markings would help (Haul Road 002, 004, 005).
- Haul Road 005-right has a wide empty grass lot between two buildings, with a blank slit-window gable wall showing.
- Haul Road 006-left and 007-left/right are large empty grass slopes with only tufts. They would take trees, a wall or a few props.
- The pavement ends in a square cut at the crest in Haul Road 006-along, with no tapering where it meets the sand-edged road in 007-along.
- Chapel Street 009-along has a large blank grey brick wall with a square notch on the left. Windows, a plaque or climbers would help.
- Chapel Street 006-left and 008-left banks are very large, plain slopes beside the road.
