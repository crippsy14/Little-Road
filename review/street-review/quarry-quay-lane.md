Kettle Falls Road otherwise looks good: clean gravel road, a nice tree line, and the gate visible at the end.

## Overall impression
Upper Quay Lane (0–170 m) is lively and well dressed, with shops, awnings, benches, lamps and flower boxes, and a busy, charming square. Its weaknesses are repetition, unreadable signs and a non-Welsh palette. The lower lane (730 m and beyond) is where the real placement bugs are: the floating house, the mid-slope walls and stubs, and the blank arch sign. The quay is big and empty. Between town and quay (around 490 m) the street is bare field.
