## Improvements (not defects)
- Shopfront signs could sit on the awning fascia or above the railing so they can be read.
- Benches sit tight under flower boxes in 005-left and 006-left; pull them forward.
- The arched carriage entrances are filled with a flat blue panel (005-left, 007-left, 011-left, 012-right); a door or a gate would read better.
- Gable-end walls beside gaps would benefit from a few details (a door, a drainpipe, a bay).
- The long terraces repeat the same few facades; 008-right and 009-right show the same blue-shuttered and green-shuttered house patterns.
- Parked cars are all nose-in to the walls on the grass strip and mostly in front of doors; spacing them to avoid doors and arches would help.
