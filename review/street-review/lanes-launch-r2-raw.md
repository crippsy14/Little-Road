# Bramblewick Lanes: launch review r2

Verdict: NOT YET, but close. The road, the start, the hairpin and the racing now look good. The ford is still a placeholder and the lap is still visually monotone between the Dairy T and the bridge.

Looked at: both sheets, plus originals ford, bridge, hairpin, race-00 and race-06 (5 of 6).

## Round 1 findings
- LR1-01 ford: PARTLY. There are now stone wing walls and a stone post. But the water is still one flat, opaque pale-blue rectangle laid on the tarmac. It has no brook, no banks and no recess, and I see no reeds or FORD board from the approach. It looks like a blue rug.
- LR1-02 bridge: FIXED. A real crest with stone parapets and a lamp is visible ahead, with a thatched cottage beyond.
- LR1-03 mirrored Mill Bend board: PARTLY. The new HUMPBACK BRIDGE text reads the right way round. But it is tiny, blue-grey on the hedge top, with speckle dots, and is unreadable at speed.
- LR1-04 wall texture and ends: NOT FIXED. The coping is still a row of vertical planks (picket look) in both the bridge and race views. Hedge ends still finish in curled stubs (ford, far left).
- LR1-05 hedgegap block: FIXED. The kerb now tapers and the hedge is lower on the inside. The stone pier with hedge stub at the ford is similar and still slightly pasted-on (see LR2-03).
- LR1-06 start: FIXED. The gantry is a dark frame (text not legible at this size, so unchecked). The grid slots are clear painted boxes and the bunting works. The start/finish line is still faint.
- LR1-07 hairpin: FIXED. In race view it has hay bales, a kerb, and the grandstand ahead. The static hairpin view still reads as a straight with the stand dead ahead. In the race it is clear.
- LR1-08 shadow seam: Not seen in these shots. Closed.

## New findings
- LR2-01 | ford | HIGH (conf. high) | The water is a flat opaque sheet with a hard straight edge. It sits on top of the road, with no ripple, no foam and no dip. It reads as a coloured rectangle, not water. The warning boards and spray are not visible from the approach.
- LR2-02 | start (race-00) | MEDIUM (conf. high) | The countdown "2" is a large 3D numeral. It floats above the car ahead and covers the road and the cars in front of the player. The HUD also shows "FINAL LAP" and "LAP 1/1" at the start of a one-lap race.
- LR2-03 | ford | LOW (conf. medium) | The left wing wall is cut short, with a stone pier and hedge stub on the grass. The right wall ends in a block. The two walls do not match.
- LR2-04 | bridge | LOW (conf. medium) | Speckle particles and a flag overlap the tree and the small sign on the hedge.
- LR2-05 | esses to millbend (7 of the static views) | MEDIUM (conf. high) | The stone bank plus hedge corridor with grey road is still nearly identical. Landmarks are tiny at distance: barn, rookery and beacon are specks, and the beacon tower is not visible at all. Gallows has a small flag only. The cider barn and orchard are not noticeable.
- LR2-06 | hairpin, race-06 | LOW (conf. medium) | The grandstand is a plain box, with a flat blue back and ends. There are no spectators visible on the terraces.
- Positives: racing traffic on the 9 m road looks right (the road holds 3 wide). Kerbs, bale walls, bunting, the thatched village and the HUD are all good.

## Top improvements, in priority order
1. Ford: make the water translucent and a little recessed or dipped, with a foam or ripple edge, a visible FORD board 100 m before, reeds, and matching wing walls.
2. Wall coping: use a smooth rounded cap, and taper or pier the hedge ends.
3. Break the corridor on 3 to 4 stretches with open gaps onto fields. Scale up the landmarks (taller barn, beacon, cider barn) and place them nearer the road.
4. Make signs bigger, in dark green on cream. The bridge board is the worst.
5. Spectators on the grandstand and at the start.
6. Fix the countdown number: smaller and screen-space. Hide "FINAL LAP" on a one-lap race.

## Verdict: NOT YET
Must-fix: LR2-01 (ford), LR1-04 (wall coping), LR2-05 (landmarks and variety, at least 3 clearly visible landmarks), LR2-02 (countdown overlay). Everything else can ship.
