## Bayview, hillcrest-mainline (BH-)

BH-1 (000-right) Lone sandstone wall block standing on its own in the middle of the lawn, right of centre, between the big pine and the pavement. It has no purpose: it isn't a fence, a bench or a plinth. The grey kerb slab at the bottom centre also ends in a square block on the grass. Severity low, confidence high.

BH-2 (001-left, 002-left, 003-left, 004-left, 005-left) Pale blue translucent vertical streaks stand in the water under the viaduct piers. They run well below each pier and look like slanted glass slabs or light shafts. Most obvious in 003-left and 004-left (centre and left of frame, under the arches), and also at the pier by the station wall in 002-left. Probably a water depth or shore-foam effect leaking below the surface. Severity medium, confidence medium.

BH-3 (004-along, 005-left, 005-right) A narrow road-marking strip crosses the carriageway just before the Hillcrest Valley gate. In 004-along it is a thin dark line across the far road, centre. In 005-left and 005-right it shows as a white-and-black strip at the near road edge, lower centre. It looks like a speed-hump or crossing marking that doesn't span the road and has no clear edge. Possible, severity low, confidence low-medium.

BH-4 (001-left) A blue van is parked tight against the station front, centre-right, in front of the door with the interaction bubble. Its roof corner appears to overlap the edge of the flower bed to its left. Possible clip, severity low, confidence low.

BH-5 (002-left) The curved railway viaduct at the left of frame dead-ends into the side of the road viaduct. The rail ring stops at the pier with no visible join. Possible, severity low, confidence low.

BH-6 (001-left) The "HILLCREST LINE" heading on the station notice board, left foreground, is tiny. It is yellow on mustard and hard to read. Severity low, confidence medium.

## Improvements (brief)

- Bayview lawns (000-right, 001-right): large bare grass with a single palm or bench. A hedge, flower border or picnic furniture would help.
- Station forecourt: add a road-side bay for the parked vans so they aren't straight in front of the door.
- The viaduct water line has no foam or ripple at the piers.
- Blind windows with dark-red shutters on the 009-right corner building's flank are a flat panel. Real glass or a painted sign would read better.
- The bay window on the pink house (009-left) is flat dark panes with no frame detail.
- Striped awnings are flat discs, with no fabric fold.
- Arcade interiors could be lit warm instead of black.
- Quay-road houses repeat the same few facades. More colour or storey variety would help.
- A tidy-up of the waterwheel with a stream, or moving it to the river edge.
