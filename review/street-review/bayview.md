What works: the town front (000-right, 001-right, 002-right: Post Office, Daily Mile cafe, Pit Stop, Tides & Tyres, terrace with bunting and parked cars) is lively and well placed; the 016-along entry view into town with the Pit Stop sign and Bayview board is a strong composition; road edges, kerbs and walls meet the ground cleanly along the loop; the sea-side wall stretch (011-014 right) with cypresses is pleasant.
