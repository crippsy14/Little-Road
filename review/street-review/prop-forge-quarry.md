# Prop Forge review, Quarry Hills (pf-quarry-1)

Reviewed about 55 of 210 photos. These were all views of Quarry Road 000-001, 008, Haul road 003 (first read failed), 008, 010, Quay Lane 004, 013, 014, High Street 010-011, Kettle Falls road 000-001 and Chapel Street 009. The rest were not reviewed. Several first reads failed with "request limit"; Quarry Road 000-001 were re-read OK. Quarry Road 002-007 and 009-012 were not reviewed, and the Haul road 003 re-read was not done.

## Verdict
Prop Forge items that appear in these photos look good. The dry-stone walls are continuous and lumpy-coped, and piers sit square and grounded. Walls follow the ground and are not buried or floating, and none cross roads, drives or buildings. The new trees (round, pine, birch, flat-top, fir) show good variety, with trunks grounded and no building or wall clipping. Rocks (standing stones in the quarry floor, mossy boulders) are grounded. No gate is visible close up, so gates are largely UNVERIFIED. The worst defects are not Prop Forge: they are Quay Lane buildings and paving floating over cuttings.

## Prop Forge findings

It may be a gate across the road, or in a gap beside it. Too distant to judge. Severity: medium if it is across the road. Confidence: low. Needs a close-up at about along 90-120 m.
PF-2 high-street/011-along and 011-right: a lone grey square pier stands by the road edge at the right, with no wall attached. It reads as a stray block. Severity low, confidence medium. This is probably the end of a wall stretch whose wall starts beyond the frame.

PF-4 quay-lane/013-left, 013-along: round bush/moss clumps sit on the steep bare rock cutting. Their shadows are offset below-left, so they look stuck on, or hovering off, the slope. Severity low, confidence medium. Same on haul-road/008-left (small green clump on the bare slope, left).
PF-5 quay-lane/013-along: dark cypress shapes at the base of both arch pillars are half hidden behind or inside the pillars. Severity low, confidence low.
PF-6 kettle-falls-road/001-right: at the right a tree trunk ends in mid-air under the near tree, with no shadow. It may be a partly hidden trunk. Severity low, confidence low.
PF-7 haul-road/010-left and 010-right: standing stones (tall thin pillars) on the quarry floor read as tombstones or menhirs. They are grounded. This is an improvement only.
