## Overall impression (partial)
Via Crest reads well: colourful, varied frontages, bunting, lamps and parked cars give the lower stretch real life, and the parapet with the valley view near the top (024–025) is a highlight. The weak spots are where the street meets the castle: a bare lawn and a hard step at the gate. Car placement (on the pavement, through a lamp) is the main bug class.
