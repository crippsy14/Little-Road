# Bramblewick Lanes: launch review r3

Looked at: both sheets, plus originals ford, beacon, bridge, toplane, gallows, start (6 of 6).

## Round 2 findings
- LR2-01 ford: FIXED. The water is now translucent, rippled and glittery, with a short wing wall each side. It no longer reads as a rug. It is still a thin strip laid flush on the road with no stream into the fields, and the FORD board is unreadable from the approach (see LR3-02). Acceptable for launch.
- LR1-04 wall coping and ends: PARTLY. The picket cope band is gone and the end piers and hedge domes look intentional. A comb of vertical slats still runs along the top course of the stone wall (toplane left, bridge both sides), so a milder picket look remains.
- LR2-05 landmarks: PARTLY (enough for launch). In the race view the red barn with bales, the grandstand with crowd and the thatched village are clearly readable. In the static views the beacon (grey tower with orange ball at gallows) is now visible, but the rookery, cider barn, mill and fingerpost are still specks or absent, and the beacon fire reads as an orange ball. The esses to toplane stretch is still the same corridor.
- LR2-02 countdown: out of scope (game-wide HUD). Still present in race-00.
- LR2-03 ford wall mismatch: FIXED (they now match).
- LR2-04 bridge speckle: not seen. Closed.
- LR2-06 grandstand: PARTLY. The crowd is in front of it, but the terraces are still empty. Low.

## New findings
- LR3-01 | bridge, toplane | MEDIUM-HIGH (conf. high) | The corner and warning boards are cropped. They read "CK BRIDGE" and "RIM COR". The text is wider than the board and the board is partly buried in the hedge, so the left letters are hidden. On toplane the board is dark grey with grey-green text and has low contrast. This is the one visible text defect on the track.
- LR3-02 | ford | LOW-MEDIUM (conf. medium) | The FORD and FORD AHEAD boards are tiny pale squares and cannot be read from the road.
- LR3-03 | start | LOW (conf. medium) | The gantry text "BRAMBLEWICK LANES" is green on grey-green. The bunting crosses it and hides the first letters.
- LR3-04 | gallows, rookery, hedgegap, ford | LOW (conf. medium) | Free-standing hedge stubs sit on the grass as long capsules with round ends, like giant sausages, with no wall behind them.
- LR3-05 | gallows | LOW (conf. low) | The beacon looks like a silo with an orange ball. It does not read as a beacon or have a visible flame.
- Positives: the crowd at the start and hairpin reads well. The bunting, grid boxes, kerbs, hay bales, thatched village and 24-car racing all look good. Hedge domes and piers are clean.

## Remaining improvements, in priority order
1. Fix the boards: size them to the text, put them clear of the hedge, use dark green on cream, and make them taller.
2. Remove the remaining slat comb on the wall top, and give the beacon a visible flame.
3. Make the FORD boards bigger. Add a stream continuing into the fields either side of the ford.
4. Add a few more large, readable props between the esses and the beacon (the mill, the cider barn, the rookery nearer the road).
5. Add spectators to the grandstand terraces. Make the gantry text high-contrast.

## Verdict: NOT YET (one small fix)
Must-fix: LR3-01, the cropped boards (the sign text is cut off and buried in the hedge). Everything else can ship. After that fix I would call it LAUNCH-READY.
