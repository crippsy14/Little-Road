# Personality race-form follow-up

23 September. Frozen86, twenty paired seeds2000–2019, forty completed solo Bayview runs. Every run uses the same club car, rear starting slot, base braking0.72 and risk5. Consistency0.9 gives a0.003 form amplitude; consistency0.2 gives0.024. Within each pair the driver ID, seed and sampled draw are identical. No excluded runs.

All40 finish normally with no contacts, road-edge samples or recovery. Steady mean24.458059s and sample SD0; variable mean24.470142s and sample SD0.026279s, range24.433060–24.524725s. The less consistent setting produces wider measured dispersion, but the effect is tiny and the steady setting has no observed finish-time variance on this course. This is evidence of a small braking-form mechanism, not convincing evidence of a lively finishing-position distribution. Do not claim otherwise in the UI or milestone review.

Keep this first slice and move to the user-prioritised preview/adventure and visual work. Broader driver skills, owned corner execution events, teammate growth and track familiarity remain future work. Do not add random force or per-frame steering noise to manufacture variance.
