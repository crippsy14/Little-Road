## Limits and next decisions

These are bounded dry-road fixtures, not whole-game balance or physical-controller acceptance. The fixed reference policy deliberately removes AI skill variation. Its cornering margins and centring are identical policies across loadouts, but its chosen inputs adapt to equipment. Speed estimates agreeing here do not validate hill, fuel, tyre-wear, wet-road or off-road estimates. Use matched full-field races and first-time player feedback to assess whether early purchases feel worthwhile.

Reference-driver times include pursuit, reaction and cornering margins; they are not ideal car-performance ceilings or production AI-skill acceptance.
At stock engine power, sport brakes do not save more than 0.05 s over sport tyres alone through this U-turn. The initial hypothesis of a larger low-power gain was falsified; do not confuse better physical stopping ability with a guaranteed useful lap-time gain on every course.
Matched faster-car brake upgrade saves 0.0417 s on this run. Evaluate that effect across later braking zones, equipment prices and player feedback before calling the upgrade well balanced.
