- Retrieved: 17 September 2026. Size: 5,451,493 bytes. Provider MD5: `2eba3a4d7eeb23cbfbeca364c97e7980`; verified after download.
- Local file: `unity/Assets/Resources/Sky/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky_2k.hdr`.
- Use: cloud sky and environment reflections. The original HDR file is unchanged; runtime sampling rotates the sky and adjusts exposure, with the directional light aligned to its brightest pixel. This is third-party photography, not original project artwork.

## Terrain materials

- [Rock 08](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_08), by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0. Original scan width 1.5m; the world-space shader can rescale its detail for the authored formations.
- [Grass Ground](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground), by Charlotte Baglioni, Poly Haven, CC0. Original scan width approximately 2.51m. The provider API records dimensions in millimetres ([API schema](https://github.com/Poly-Haven/Public-API/blob/master/swagger.yml)).
- Each uses the 2K diffuse JPG, OpenGL normal PNG and AO/roughness/metal JPG from the provider. Downloaded 17 September 2026; every file was checked against its provider MD5. Exact source URLs, sizes, MD5 and SHA256 are recorded in `review/unity-09/material-downloads.json` alongside the source API metadata.
- Local files: `unity/Assets/Resources/Surfaces/rock_08` and `unity/Assets/Resources/Surfaces/grass_ground`. Source images are unchanged. Unity imports normals as normal maps and scalar maps as linear data; the runtime terrain shader changes mapping, tint and surface blending. These are third-party photographic surface maps.
- The authored roadside/bridge masonry study also uses Rock 08 diffuse and normal maps with a separate UV scale, restrained normal strength and varied limestone tints. Its chamfered stone geometry is generated by the project; the surface photography retains the credit above.
- The coastal relief revision uses the same credited Rock 08 surface on original fractured outcrops and fallen blocks. Their meshes are generated by the project from authored profiles and intersecting cutting planes; they are not derived from the optional coastal-cliff scan below.

## Coastal Cliff 01 — comparison build

- Provider: [Poly Haven Coastal Cliff 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/coastal_cliff_01).
- Authors: Rob Tuytel (photography/processing), Rico Cilliers (cleanup).
- Licence: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).
- Retrieved 19 September 2026: 2K Blender source plus diffuse JPG, OpenGL normal EXR and roughness EXR. All four provider MD5 checks passed. Exact URLs and checksums are in `references/coastal-assets/coastal_cliff_01-downloads.json`; mesh inventory is adjacent.
- Local source: `source-art/vendor/polyhaven/coastal_cliff_01`. Vendor source is unmodified. Checkpoint 11 and the preceding coast-art review do not contain it.
- Comparison derivative: `source-art/export_cliff_study.py` clips the central 24 m, preserves UVs and creates three render LODs (30,447 / 15,311 / 3,674 triangles), the last simplified from the provider's LOD3. FBX exports are in `source-art/derived/coastal-cliff-study`, with the export inventory. The scene comparison is opt-in via `--cliff-study`.
- Unity comparison assets: `unity/Assets/Resources/CoastStudy`. `CliffStudyImport` converts the roughness map into a linear metallic/smoothness PNG (metallic zero, alpha = 1 - roughness); normal and colour source maps are unchanged. A serialized Lit material retains the normal/gloss shader variants; `--cliff-photo-surface` selects it. The default study instead applies the credited Rock 08 world-space limestone surface used elsewhere in the landscape. Runtime geometry is vertically scaled, its cropped ends bend into the slope, and its uneven base is fitted to the local ground using a shared LOD0 profile. The high-detail render mesh is also the static mesh collider; all its transformed vertices are checked against protected road/village/lighthouse areas. This remains an evaluation, not final geometry or performance acceptance.

The car, village, base landscape and vegetation meshes remain project-generated. The optional comparison cliff is third-party scan geometry. Engine/software licences are tracked separately in the Windows distribution.
