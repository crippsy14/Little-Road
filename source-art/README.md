# Editable source art

`little_car.blend` is the editable original car source. `build_car.py` reproducibly creates it with Blender 5.2.2 and exports `../game/assets/little_car.glb`.

The car has a body paint material and four independent wheel pivots, imported and animated in Godot. Its proportions and detail remain preliminary; compare the in-game captures with the concept sheets before further modelling.
